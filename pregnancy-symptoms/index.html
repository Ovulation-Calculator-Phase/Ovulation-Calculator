After the fertile window

Early pregnancy symptoms

A missed period, breast tenderness, nausea and fatigue can have many causes. Symptoms alone cannot confirm pregnancy.

When to take a test

Follow your home test instructions. Testing after a missed period is often more informative than testing very early. If the result is negative but your period does not arrive, repeat the test as directed or contact a clinician.

Get urgent care for warning signs

Seek urgent medical care for severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding, shoulder pain, fainting or feeling seriously unwell, especially after a positive test.
