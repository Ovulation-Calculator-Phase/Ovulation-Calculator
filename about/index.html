About the site

Simple cycle planning, clearly explained.

Ovulation Calculator provides a calm, private way to explore estimated cycle dates and learn where calendar methods have limits.

What we provide

The calculator uses the first day of a period, a typical cycle length and an estimated luteal phase to produce educational dates. Supporting pages explain symptoms, tracking methods, fertility evaluation and pregnancy testing in plain English.

What we do not provide

We do not diagnose, prescribe, guarantee conception or replace a clinician. Content is reviewed for clarity and links to established medical organizations, but it is not individual medical advice.

Privacy by design

Calculations run in your browser. No account is required. Read the privacy policy and health disclaimer before using the site.
