Important limits

Health disclaimer

This site provides general educational information and calendar estimates, not medical advice.

What results mean

Calculator results are estimates based on information you enter. They cannot diagnose a condition, confirm ovulation, guarantee conception, determine pregnancy, replace contraception or predict a date with certainty.

When to contact a professional

Talk with a qualified healthcare professional about personal symptoms, medications, contraception, pregnancy, fertility concerns or irregular cycles. Seek urgent care for severe pain, heavy bleeding, fainting, trouble breathing or other emergency symptoms.

External sources

Links to medical organizations are provided for further reading. External content may change and does not create a clinician-patient relationship with this site.
