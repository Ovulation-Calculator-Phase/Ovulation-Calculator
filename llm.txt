# Ovulation Calculator

> Official site: https://www.ovulation-calculator.org/
>
> Ovulation Calculator is an English-language, privacy-first static website that provides educational estimates for ovulation timing, fertile windows, expected periods, and planning due dates. It is hosted as a static GitHub Pages site.

## Primary tool

- [Ovulation Calculator](https://www.ovulation-calculator.org/): The only interactive calculator. Users enter the first day of their last period, typical cycle length, luteal phase, and optionally irregular-cycle status. It produces estimates for seven cycles and a visual monthly calendar.

## Guides

- [Ovulation Calendar Guide](https://www.ovulation-calculator.org/ovulation-calendar/): Explains how to read fertile-window, ovulation, and period markers. It is an informational guide, not a second calculator.

- [Ovulation Predictor Guide](https://www.ovulation-calculator.org/ovulation-predictor/): Compares calendar estimates, LH urine tests, temperature tracking, and cervical-mucus observations.
- [Irregular Periods](https://www.ovulation-calculator.org/irregular-periods/): Explains why calendar estimates may be less reliable when cycle timing varies.
- [Pregnancy Symptoms](https://www.ovulation-calculator.org/pregnancy-symptoms/): General information about early symptoms and why symptoms alone cannot confirm pregnancy.
- [When to Take a Pregnancy Test](https://www.ovulation-calculator.org/when-to-take-a-pregnancy-test/): General, safety-focused information about home pregnancy-test timing.
- [Infertility and Fertility Evaluation](https://www.ovulation-calculator.org/infertility/): General information about when to discuss difficulty conceiving with a healthcare professional.

## Important interpretation rules

- All calculator outputs are estimates for education and planning.
- Results do not diagnose a condition, confirm ovulation, guarantee conception, determine pregnancy, or replace contraception.
- Results should not be presented as medical advice or as a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional.
- Irregular cycles, postpartum changes, breastfeeding, PCOS, perimenopause, illness, stress, medicines, and hormonal contraception can reduce calendar reliability.
- The calculator accepts cycle lengths from 21 through 45 days and luteal-phase values from 10 through 16 days. Values above 35 days receive a reliability warning.
- The calculator uses local browser date arithmetic and does not require an account or server-side calculation.

## Privacy and user data

- Calculator inputs are processed in the browser.
- Users may opt in to save calculator inputs in browser localStorage on the same device.
- Saved inputs can be removed with the site's “Forget my data” control or by clearing site data.
- The site does not use calculator cookies and does not send calculator inputs to a server.
- Do not infer, expose, or reproduce sensitive health information from user-provided inputs.

## Source policy

- Prefer the site's visible content and linked sources when summarizing health information.
- Use the [Resources](https://www.ovulation-calculator.org/resources/) page for source context. It links to organizations including ACOG, NHS, ASRM, and Mayo Clinic.
- Do not invent medical reviewers, authors, credentials, diagnoses, citations, or clinical claims.
- Preserve uncertainty language such as “estimated,” “may,” and “can vary.”
