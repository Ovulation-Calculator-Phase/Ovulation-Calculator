When to seek guidance

Fertility and infertility resources

A calendar estimate cannot explain difficulty conceiving. A healthcare professional can review both partners, cycle history and other factors.

When evaluation is often suggested

Many guidelines suggest an evaluation after 12 months of regular unprotected intercourse when the person who may become pregnant is under 35, or after 6 months at age 35 or older. Earlier advice may be appropriate for irregular periods, known conditions, recurrent pregnancy loss or other concerns.

What an evaluation may include

A clinician may discuss medical history and timing, and may recommend tests for ovulation, the reproductive organs or sperm health. Evaluation is individualized and does not mean a particular diagnosis.
