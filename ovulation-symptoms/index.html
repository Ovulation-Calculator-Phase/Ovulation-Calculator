Ovulation symptoms and signs

Body changes can add context to a calendar estimate, but no single symptom confirms that ovulation happened.

Common signs to observe

Cervical mucus: Discharge often becomes wetter, clearer and more slippery near the fertile window. Basal body temperature: A small sustained rise may appear after ovulation, so charting usually confirms a pattern afterward. LH tests: Urine tests detect a luteinizing hormone surge that commonly occurs before ovulation.

Other symptoms

Some people notice breast tenderness, bloating, increased libido or brief one-sided pelvic discomfort. These symptoms are not specific and may have other causes.

When to seek care

Contact a healthcare professional for severe or persistent pelvic pain, heavy or unusual bleeding, fever, fainting, or concerns about fertility. Illness, sleep changes, medicines, stress and breastfeeding can affect observations.
