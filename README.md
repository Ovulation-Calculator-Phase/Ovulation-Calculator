# Ovulation Calculator

[Ovulation Calculator](https://www.ovulation-calculator.org/) helps estimate ovulation timing, the fertile window, the next period, and an estimated due date from menstrual-cycle information.

This calculator is intended for education and planning. Its results are estimates, not medical diagnoses or guarantees of pregnancy.

## How to Use the Calculator

1. Enter the first day of your last menstrual period.
2. Choose your usual cycle length.
3. Choose a luteal-phase length if you know it. The default is 14 days.
4. Select `My cycles are irregular` if your cycles do not follow a consistent pattern.
5. Select `Remember my details on this device` only if you want to save your inputs in that browser on that device.
6. Select `Show my fertile window`.

The calculator displays estimates for the next seven cycles.

## Calculator Inputs

### First day of your last period

Use the first day of your most recent menstrual bleeding, not the final day of bleeding or the day you notice light spotting.

### Cycle length

The calculator offers cycle lengths from 21 through 45 days. The default is 28 days. Cycles longer than 35 days can still be calculated, but the site displays a warning because calendar estimates may be less reliable.

### Luteal phase

The luteal-phase setting ranges from 10 through 16 days, with a default of 14 days. If you do not know your luteal-phase length, use the default and remember that the resulting dates are estimates.

## What the Results Show

### Estimated ovulation

The estimated date of ovulation based on the selected cycle length and luteal phase.

### Fertile window

An estimated range covering the five days before the estimated ovulation date through the day after it.

### Expected period

The estimated start of the next period based on the last-period date and selected cycle length.

### Estimated due date

A planning estimate based on the last-period date. It does not confirm pregnancy and may change with clinical information.

### Seven-cycle calendar

Results are also shown in a monthly calendar. Markers identify the period start, fertile window, and estimated ovulation.

## How the Estimate Works

The calculator uses simple calendar formulas:

- Expected next period = first day of the last period + cycle length.
- Estimated ovulation = expected next period − luteal phase.
- Fertile window = five days before estimated ovulation through the following day.
- Estimated due date = first day of the last period + 280 days.

Menstrual cycles can change. Stress, illness, weight or exercise changes, travel, breastfeeding, PCOS, perimenopause, medicines, and hormonal contraception can affect ovulation timing.

## Remembering Your Inputs

`Remember my details on this device` is optional.

- Inputs are stored only in the browser's `localStorage` on the device you use.
- Inputs are not sent to a server for calculation.
- Calculator results are not stored as a history.
- Use `Forget my data` to remove saved inputs.
- Do not enable this feature on a shared device if you do not want other users to see your cycle information.

No account is required to use the calculator.

## Theme and Installation

The site provides light and dark modes. The initial mode follows your browser or operating-system preference. You can switch modes with the theme button.

On supported browsers, the site can be installed as a web app. On an iPhone or iPad, use the Share menu and select Add to Home Screen.

## When to Seek Help

Speak with a healthcare professional if you have very irregular cycles, severe or persistent pelvic pain, unusual bleeding, or difficulty conceiving.

Seek urgent medical care for severe pain, heavy bleeding, fainting, difficulty breathing, or any other worrying emergency symptoms.

## Health Disclaimer

Ovulation Calculator provides general information and calendar-based estimates. It does not diagnose health conditions, confirm ovulation, confirm pregnancy, guarantee conception, prevent pregnancy, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
