Trust and standards

Editorial policy

Ovulation Calculator is a brand-run educational site. We explain our formulas, link to established health organizations, and state the limits of calendar estimates plainly.

How content is created

Content is written for general education and reviewed for clarity, consistency with the calculator, and cautious health language. The site does not currently have a named medical reviewer. It does not claim to diagnose, prescribe, or replace a healthcare professional.

Sources and updates

We use public information from organizations such as ACOG, NHS, ASRM, and Mayo Clinic. We update pages when the calculation method, source guidance, or site behavior changes.

Corrections

Report a factual, accessibility, or broken-link issue at hello@ovulation-calculator.org. Include the page URL and the specific issue, but do not send private health information.

External links

Links to external sources are provided for further reading. External pages have their own content and policies, and a link does not create a clinician-patient relationship.
